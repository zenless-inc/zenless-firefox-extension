One more step: allow access to all sites

Your browser installed the extension without site access. Zenless needs it to catch magnet links, pass cookies for logged-in downloads and collect links from pages. It never reads or sends your browsing data anywhere.

What it does

Download capture

Archives, programs, videos and documents open in Zenless Download Manager's "New download" dialog instead of the browser. If the app isn't running, the browser simply downloads as usual.

Magnet links & .torrent files

Click a magnet link or a .torrent file and Zenless Torrent asks which files to get and where to save them.

Right-click menu

"Download with Zenless" on any link, "Download all links" on a page or a selection, "Download media" on images and videos, and "Download with browser instead" when you want the usual behavior.

Media on the page

Videos and songs a page plays show up in the toolbar popup with a Download button, and the icon shows how many were found.

Pause when you need to

Turn capture off, or pause it for five minutes from the popup. Exclude sites and choose file types in settings.

Private by design

The extension only talks to the Zenless apps on your own computer (127.0.0.1). No accounts, no analytics, nothing sent to the internet.
